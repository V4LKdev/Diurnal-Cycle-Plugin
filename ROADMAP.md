# Roadmap

Current follow-up priorities are tracked in the
[documentation roadmap](https://v4lkdev.github.io/Diurnal-Cycle-Plugin/roadmap/).

The main goals are a verified Unreal Engine 5.8 Linux bundle, real editor and
Blueprint screenshots, direct Timeline manipulation, additional calendar views,
and careful evaluation of asynchronous schedule activation.

These items are desired work, not compatibility or release date promises.
